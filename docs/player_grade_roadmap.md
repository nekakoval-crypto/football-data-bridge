# PBK Player Grade implementation roadmap

1. Foundation: source-normalization, aggregate research grade, event-level prototype, Form 5/10, XI Quality, Manual Lineup WHAT-IF contract.
2. Projection: store captured per-match player stats and immutable pre-kickoff XI-grade snapshots in Stage72-compatible tables/read models.
3. Match Card: show player grade/confidence beneath each player on the mirrored pitch and surface expected-to-official/manual replacement deltas.
4. Manual Configurator UI: select fixture, edit both XIs/formations, attach insider note, save/rerun named scenarios.
5. Research audit: Player Grade/Form/Importance/XI Quality versus market expectation, xG/result and Forward/WATCH observations with sample-size warnings.
6. Promotion gate: no model feature or canonical impact until independent forward validation demonstrates stable incremental information.
